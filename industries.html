Featured Projects

A glimpse into our successfully executed engineering challenges

Automotive Assembly Line Automation

Designed, manufactured, and commissioned a fully automated assembly line for a generic automotive component manufacturer. The project involved integrating multiple workstations, robotic handling, and vision inspection systems to achieve a cycle time of 12 seconds.

Key Deliverables:
  • 100% Automated Workflow
  • Integrated Quality Control
  • Real-time Production Monitoring

Robotic Welding Integration

Executed a turnkey robotic welding cell project for heavy structural fabrication. The solution included a 6-axis robot on a linear track, synchronized with a dual-axis positioner for complex weld geometries.

Key Deliverables:
  • Consistent Weld Quality
  • 300% Efficiency Increase
  • Enhanced Operator Safety
